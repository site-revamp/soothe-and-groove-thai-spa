# Soothe & Groove Thai Spa – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Soothe & Groove Thai Spa is a massage and wellness spa located at Clover Bay Tower, Business Bay, Dubai. The business offers traditional Thai massage, relaxation therapies, hot stone treatments, couples massage, Moroccan bath rituals, and multi-therapist experiences.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting a consistent spa identity, making treatment options easier to understand, and creating a clearer journey from discovering services to contacting the spa.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Brand Messaging
- Removed the conflicting reference to "Fairy Land Spa" from the homepage content.
- Established Soothe & Groove Thai Spa as the consistent business identity throughout the presentation.
- Improved brand messaging to create a clearer and more trustworthy first impression.

### 2. Better Treatment Presentation
- Organised massage therapies and spa experiences into clearer service sections.
- Improved the presentation of individual treatments to help visitors understand the available options.
- Created a more structured treatment discovery experience, making it easier to explore different massage and wellness services.

### 3. Improved Booking and Contact Journey
- Made Call and WhatsApp contact options more visible.
- Improved the presentation of essential business information, including the Business Bay location and address.
- Structured booking-related information more clearly to help visitors understand how to enquire and plan a spa visit.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Applied the approved gold (#EFC17C), black, and white colour palette to create a refined luxury spa appearance.
- **Layout:** Improved section organisation, spacing, alignment, and content hierarchy.
- **Typography:** Established a consistent typographic hierarchy for headings, treatment descriptions, and supporting content.
- **User Experience:** Simplified treatment discovery and improved access to contact and booking options.
- **Consistency:** Standardised brand messaging, colours, buttons, and service presentation across the homepage.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a more cohesive and polished homepage that reflects the spa's Thai massage and luxury wellness positioning.
